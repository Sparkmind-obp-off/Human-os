## A. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### What Human-OS is (evidence-derived)

Human-OS is a **standalone, human-centered AI operating layer** designed to help one person think, organize, communicate, decide, and execute across life and work through a **persistent, voice-first interface**. It is not a chat agent, not a companion, not an OS in the Windows/macOS sense, and not a replacement for human relationships. Its operating loop is: **Sense → Understand → Plan → Act → Verify → Remember → Reflect.** (Source: `README.md`, `docs/00_PROJECT_CHARTER.md`, `docs/01_PRODUCT_DEFINITION.md`.)

Human-OS is architecturally distinct from **Runner OS** and **Business OS**; connectors are external. The initial target user is an individual operator/founder already using multiple digital systems, with a phased roadmap that ends in a multi-user, enterprise-grade, workspace-isolated, billable platform (`docs/11_ROADMAP_AND_PHASE_GATES.md`).

### Naming strategy used

Ten naming territories were surveyed (abstract human-centered, Latinate/Greek-inspired, obscure real-word roots, modern open-vowel, intelligence/agency/coordination, human+system, cognition/action/orchestration, subtle language-root, premium software constructions, short invented). Fifty-four candidates were generated across three research waves. Rapid elimination, live web research, phonetic/human tests, primary-association tests, adversarial testing, and 100-point internal scoring were applied throughout.

### Candidates considered

54 candidates generated. 50 eliminated with documented evidence. 4 candidates survived to the final adversarial gate as a **watchlist**, but none passed the full final gate defined in §17 of the Master Prompt.

### Major elimination patterns observed

Three systemic patterns dominated the elimination log:

1. **The AI/operating-layer naming space is saturated.** Multiple strong-sounding invented names already belong to companies positioned as *"AI operating system,"* *"operating layer,"* *"AI-native operating layer,"* or *"agent operating system"* — the exact positioning of Human-OS. Examples: **Halden HQ** (haldenhq.com — *"operating layer for founder-led companies"*), **Corven OS** (corvenos.com — *"AI Operating System"*), **Emryn** (AI/ML enterprise solutions), **Solven** (multiple AI agencies globally), **Lumeon** (care-orchestration platform, now Health Catalyst), **Praxen** (Exabeam AI-agent behavior verification), **AlterOS** (Russian operating system).
2. **Invented 2-syllable names ending in -en / -eon / -ora / -ith / -yx are essentially "picked over."** Every such construction that was tested had at least one active company, consumer brand, or fictional franchise using it.
3. **Even "obscure" invented candidates carry surname, place-name, gaming-boss, or foreign-place associations** that would dominate a stranger's first interpretation and therefore fail the Primary Association Test (§11).

### Final decision

## **NO BRAND LOCK.**

No candidate produced in this cycle satisfies every gate criterion in §17 with documented evidence. A follow-up cycle is defined in §I below, with a narrower construction rulebook and additional research requirements.

## B. REPOSITORY-DERIVED NAMING BRIEF

Derived exclusively from files in `Sparkmind-obp-off/Human-os` — no assumptions from filenames or prior conversations.

### B.1 Product definition
A persistent, voice-first personal operating layer for reasoning, coordination, memory, and action across life and work. Source: `docs/01_PRODUCT_DEFINITION.md`.

### B.2 Target user
Initial: individual operator/founder already using many digital systems. Future: teams, workspaces, and enterprise governance (`docs/00_PROJECT_CHARTER.md`, `docs/11_ROADMAP_AND_PHASE_GATES.md`).

### B.3 Core job-to-be-done
*"I can call [Human-OS] and say what I need; it understands the context, coordinates the right tools, performs safe actions, and brings important decisions back to me."* (`README.md`, North Star.)

### B.4 Product personality (evidence-based)
- Human agency first.
- Voice-first, not voice-only.
- Calm, respectful, non-manipulative.
- Transparent about autonomy levels (L0–L5, `docs/01_PRODUCT_DEFINITION.md`).
- Trust-oriented, safety-forward, verification before consequential action (`docs/07_SAFETY_PRIVACY_AND_TRUST.md`).
- Not a companion, not a persona, not a "friend."

### B.5 Category position
Personal AI operating layer / orchestration surface. Adjacent to — but not identical with — AI assistant, agent platform, workflow automation, personal-productivity SaaS.

### B.6 Important capabilities (naming-relevant)
Voice presence, persistent memory with permissions, connector orchestration, tool selection, risk classification, approval workflows, audit, kill-switch, background execution (`docs/03_SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/04_VOICE_AND_PRESENCE_SPEC.md`).

### B.7 Future expansion (must be name-supportable)
Personal → pro operator → team/workspace → usage-based billing → premium connector marketplace → enterprise workspace isolation with governance (`docs/10_VALIDATION_AND_BUSINESS_MODEL.md`, `docs/11_ROADMAP_AND_PHASE_GATES.md`).

### B.8 Naming implications (must-haves)
1. Must work **without** the words *AI, OS, Agent, Bot, Automation, Assistant*.
2. Must sound **premium, calm, and technically serious** — not toylike, gimmicky, or crypto-like.
3. Must be **speakable in Indonesian and English** without constant correction (the operator context is Indonesia-anchored with global ambitions).
4. Must scale from **single-user tool to enterprise workspace** without renaming.
5. Must not read as a **persona / friend / companion** name (violates §7 Dependency Guardrails).
6. Must not read as a **feature name** (Voice, Copilot, Assistant, Buddy, Helper).
7. Must be **short enough to say out loud** in the launch phrase: *"Call [NAME]."*, and **long enough to be distinctive.**
8. Must be **auditable by search** — a stranger typing the name into Google should not land in a dominant unrelated brand.

### B.9 Naming anti-patterns (must-avoids)
- Any variant of *"Human"*, *"Mind"*, *"Brain"*, *"Second Brain"* → generic and crowded.
- Any *–GPT, –AI, –Bot, –Agent, –OS, –Ops* suffix → cliché and legally hazardous.
- Names that suggest emotional/companion relationship → violates `docs/02_HUMAN_NEEDS_MODEL.md` and `docs/07_SAFETY_PRIVACY_AND_TRUST.md`.
- Casino / crypto / gambling connotations.
- Names that sound like existing famous tech brands (Vercel, Notion, Linear, etc.).
- Names identical to a known fictional character or gaming-franchise boss.
- Surname-shaped names (Bendric-, Tulbrix-, Volnith-, Andralt-type patterns already showed weakness in prior cycles per Master Prompt §19).

## I. NEXT CYCLE — CYCLE 002 PLAN

Rather than repeat Cycle 001's approach and expect a different outcome, Cycle 002 is designed around what Cycle 001's evidence proved.

### I.1 What Cycle 001 proved

- 2-syllable invented names ending in **-en / -eon / -ora / -ith / -yx** are functionally exhausted in the AI/software space.
- Positioning language *"operating layer,"* *"AI operating system,"* *"agent OS,"* *"orchestration platform"* is already claimed by multiple named competitors (Halden HQ, Corven OS, Lumeon, Emryn, AlterOS). Naming should **not** invite comparison via cognate-sound.
- Surname-shaped names (Torhan, Ostrun, Ivarn, Havren) all read as people, not brands — which specifically violates the Human-OS non-persona principle in `docs/04_VOICE_AND_PRESENCE_SPEC.md`.

### I.2 Cycle 002 construction rulebook

1. **Minimum 3 syllables** (to break out of the saturated 2-syllable AI-namespace shelf).
2. **Prefer vowel-rich, open-ending constructions** (*-a, -o, -ai*) — easier for Bahasa Indonesia speakers to say and spell after single hearing.
3. **Draw on Indonesian, Sanskrit, Javanese, or Old-Malay roots** that describe *attention, presence, calm, continuity, or agency* — anchoring the brand in the founder's local context while remaining pronounceable internationally.
4. **Ban** any *Z-* opening (over-saturated), any *-OS / -AI / -bot / -run / -flow / -sync* close (feature-word cliché), and any name that reads as a first name or surname.
5. **Force adversarial audit before web research** — every candidate must survive an in-team "does this sound like a person / a game / a supplement / a hotel?" pass before spending web-research budget.
6. **Require WHOIS + trademark database evidence before a candidate reaches the finalist stage**, not after.

### I.3 Cycle 002 seed territories (not candidates)

- Sanskrit *"Sādhaka"* family (practitioner / one who accomplishes) — e.g. rework toward a modern coined form.
- Old-Malay *"kandaga"* (treasury / repository) family — modern coined form.
- Javanese *"pamong"* (custodian, one who cares-for) family — modern coined form.
- Latin *"praesidium"* (garrison, protection, guardianship) — modern coined form.
- Greek *"episteme + agora"* (knowledge + meeting-place) — modern coined form.

These are **territories**, not names. Cycle 002 will generate names within them, apply Cycle 002's stricter rulebook, and re-test.
