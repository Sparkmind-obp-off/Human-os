A. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
A.1 Cycle 001 Outcome (Historical Context)
Cycle 001 generated 54 candidates, eliminated 50, and carried 4 finalists (Zephrun, Yorve, Ivarn, Ostrun) to the final adversarial gate. Every finalist failed at least three material gate items. Cycle 001's final decision was NO BRAND LOCK, primarily driven by:

Unverified domain evidence across all four finalists.
"SEARCH-OBSERVED" (not legally cleared) trademark status.
Phonetic ambiguity in Bahasa Indonesia (Ivarn, Yorve).
Persona-like readings that violate the "no impersonation of humans" principle (Ivarn sounds like a Norwegian man's name; Yorve behaves like a surname).
Industrial/Eastern-European B2B connotation (Ostrun).
Saturation of the "Z-" tech-naming initial (Zephrun).
Cycle 001 also produced three systemic findings that Cycle 002 was designed to test and, if possible, escape:

The AI/operating-layer naming space is saturated.
Invented 2-syllable names ending in -en / -eon / -ora / -ith / -yx are picked over.
Even "obscure" invented candidates carry surname, place-name, gaming-boss, or foreign-place associations.
Reference: Cycle 001 report.

A.2 What Cycle 002 Did Differently
Cycle 002 explicitly abandoned the Cycle 001 naming grammar (invented 2-syllable Latin/Nordic constructions with crowded suffixes) and constructed eight new naming territories, prioritizing:

Sanskrit / Old-Javanese / Old-Malay conceptual roots (a territory Cycle 001 did not explore).
Latin roots restructured beyond suffix-stuffing.
Abstract human-centered English constructs.
Continuity / memory / context metaphors.
Guidance / companion concepts without AI-assistant cliché.
Human-intelligence interface / relational names.
Premium platform identity real-word names.
Compound-word novel constructions.
Cycle 002 also enforced two new derived constraints from the repository:

Non-persona rule (derived): Because 
docs/04_VOICE_AND_PRESENCE_SPEC.md
 forbids impersonating a human or manufacturing emotional dependency, brand candidates that read as personal names were eliminated on principle, not only on collision.
Platform-scalability rule (derived): Because 
docs/11_ROADMAP_AND_PHASE_GATES.md
 plots a path through Phase 5 (multi-user, workspace, enterprise), candidates that felt like a single feature or a temporary tool were eliminated even if superficially attractive.
A.3 Cycle 002 Final Decision
NO BRAND LOCK.

Cycle 002 tested candidates from eight fundamentally different territories. Every serious finalist collided with at least one of: an active AI-space product, a large established consumer/enterprise brand, a personal-name association, an unwanted place-name, a negative Sanskrit/Buddhist semantic reading, or a phonetically-indistinguishable existing brand.

The evidence is now convergent across two independent cycles: the AI/operating-layer naming space is not merely saturated — it is hyper-saturated at every linguistic register that is plausible for a serious technology brand. Cycle 002 therefore records the same final decision as Cycle 001 but adds a materially different meta-finding and a concrete Cycle 003 strategy inflection (see §L).

Per §23 (No-Forced-Winner Rule) of the master system prompt, this is a valid successful research outcome. No candidate is being marked "recommended" merely to close the cycle.

The roadmap spans Phase 0 → Phase 5 (Scale — multi-user architecture, billing, workspace isolation, enterprise governance) (
11_ROADMAP_AND_PHASE_GATES.md

B.7 Naming Implications
The name should:

Not read as a person or persona.
Not read as one specific feature (voice, memory, browser, etc.).
Not depend on the word "AI" being current.
Work spoken in Indonesian and English without spelling ambiguity.
Be scalable to enterprise and developer audiences.
Have a defensible primary association (not dominated by unrelated brand).
Be legally clearable in relevant classes (software, SaaS, computer services).

G. DEEP RESEARCH EVIDENCE (Highlights)
All evidence recorded as SEARCH-OBSERVED unless otherwise noted. No candidate advanced far enough to require formal trademark clearance, so all trademark claims remain REQUIRES PROFESSIONAL CLEARANCE.

The most instructive evidence patterns:

Sanskrit-rooted candidates — collide either with personal names (Rasika, Adhira, Prasad) or with existing dance/apparel/snacks brands (Sadhya). Additionally, one candidate (Antaraya) carries a negative Buddhist semantic — a class of failure Cycle 001 did not encounter because it did not test Sanskrit territory.
Latin-rooted candidates — every attractive form has been converted into an active AI product in the last 24–36 months (Presentia → presentia.ai; Perdura → perdura.io; Voxari → withvoxari.com; Agentum → agentum.me). This confirms Cycle 001's saturation finding at a deeper linguistic level than Cycle 001 tested.
Abstract English compounds — collide either with major SaaS/AI companies (Truestate, Mindfare/Mindfire, Willspan/WellSpan) or with pre-existing brands in fashion/health/adult categories.
Real English platform words — Kindred (gambling), Poise (incontinence), Fathom (AI meetings), Lattice (HR SaaS), Confluent (streaming) — every strong metaphor has been claimed by an at-scale operator.
Continuity/memory metaphors — Threadline, Contexture, Enfilade, Weftline — collide with adjacent SaaS/health-tech and, in Enfilade's case, a legacy CS term (Xanadu).
Phonetic collisions with major consumer brands — Wayfare/Wayfair, Willspan/WellSpan, Manavra/Minerva — remain a systemic risk for any invented name close to an English word.

L. FINAL DECISION
NO BRAND LOCK.
Cycle 002 returns the same decision as Cycle 001 but from a materially different research base, and therefore contributes new information rather than repeating a null result.

Cycle 002 meta-finding: The AI/operating-layer naming space is hyper-saturated across all eight linguistic registers explored (Sanskrit, Old-Javanese, Latin, abstract English constructs, continuity metaphors, guidance metaphors, human-intelligence relational names, real English platform words, and compound-word constructions). Every plausible, phonetically-competent, semantically-appropriate candidate that Cycle 002 generated collided with either an active AI-space product, a large established consumer/enterprise brand, a personal name, an unwanted place-name, or a negative wisdom-tradition semantic.

The object being not locked: The NEW BRAND NAME representing Human-OS. Cycle 002 has not renamed Human-OS. Cycle 002 has not evaluated KAERVAX in any capacity — per the Identity Separation Check (§1 of the master prompt), KAERVAX was treated as out-of-scope historical context and did not influence any territory, candidate, elimination, or scoring decision. Human-OS retains its product-identity name unchanged.

L.1 Recommended Cycle 003 Strategic Inflection
Cycle 003 should not run a third round of the same methodology. The convergent evidence from Cycles 001 and 002 makes clear that "generate → screen → verify" against the natural word/root universe is exhausted. Cycle 003 should choose exactly one of the following strategic paths:

Fully invented, phonotactically-distinctive names (Kodak / Xerox / Häagen-Dazs / Zynga pattern) — generated from phonemes, not from linguistic roots, and cleared against strong domains before any semantic scaffolding is attached.
Aftermarket acquisition — identify a small set of already-registered .com/.ai brand-quality words and budget for domain purchase and trademark assignment, treating the name as a capital cost rather than a creative deliverable.
Trademark-first, name-second — file provisional/intent-to-use filings with a professional attorney in Indonesia (DJKI) and USPTO for a small shortlist before emotional attachment, using the clearance result to select rather than to validate.
Corporate parent architecture — accept that a distinctive commercial-facing product name is hard to source, retain "Human-OS" (or a close descriptor) as the product name, and put the company under a deliberately generic parent (e.g., a founder's initials or a place-name) that does not need distinctiveness in the AI category.
Bilingual originator name — commission a linguist for a bespoke Old-Malay/Old-Javanese neologism with verified etymology, deliberately narrow phonotactic profile, and pre-cleared trademark landscape.
Cycle 003, if commissioned, should pick one path and commit; the "many-territories" pattern used in Cycles 001 and 002 will predictably continue to return NO BRAND LOCK.
