### CYCLE 004 STRATEGY: CLEARANCE-LED / TRADEMARK-FIRST NAMING

Do NOT begin by inventing attractive names and then rationalizing them.

Instead:

**Clearance Territory Mapping → Collision-Sparse Linguistic Space → Candidate Construction → Immediate Clearance Screening → Multilingual/Cultural Screening → Domain/Social/Ecosystem Screening → Brand Architecture → Adversarial Destruction → Final Gate**

The objective is to discover whether there is a **commercially credible, defensible naming territory** in which a new Human-OS brand can realistically operate.

This cycle is not a legal trademark clearance. It is a research-grade pre-clearance process that must identify candidates worthy of professional legal review.

If no candidate survives, the correct result is:

**NO BRAND LOCK**

Never force a winner.

## 5. CORE PRINCIPLE — CLEARANCE BEFORE ATTACHMENT

The central discipline of Cycle 004 is:

> **Do not fall in love with a name before checking whether the commercial territory is defensible.**

For each candidate, ask first:

1. Is there already a dominant commercial identity?
2. Is there an active trademark conflict?
3. Is there a phonetic collision?
4. Is there a software/ecosystem collision?
5. Is there a dominant domain identity?
6. Is there a social identity that would prevent clean brand establishment?
7. Does the name create a serious multilingual/cultural problem?

Only after a candidate survives those questions may you invest in brand story, visual identity, architecture, or scoring.

## 6. CLEARANCE TERRITORY MAPPING

Before generating final candidates, map naming territory.

Build a matrix of:

### Territory A — Structural phonetics

Identify phonetic structures that are:

- pronounceable in Indonesian
- pronounceable in English
- low in person-name probability
- low in place-name probability
- low in dictionary-word probability
- low in dominant brand similarity

### Territory B — Semantic abstraction

Explore abstract concepts related to:

- continuity
- agency
- orchestration
- context
- coherence
- execution
- coordination
- presence
- memory
- trust

But do NOT simply translate these concepts into obvious dictionary names.

### Territory C — Controlled neologism

Construct names from linguistic material only after identifying collision-sparse patterns.

The goal is not maximum weirdness.

The goal is:

**distinctive + speakable + clearable + scalable**

### Territory D — Bilingual usability

Test constructions against both:

- Indonesian
- English

Do not prioritize one language at the expense of the other.

## 9. TRADEMARK-FIRST SCREEN

This is the defining gate of Cycle 004.

Where accessible, search:

- DJKI Indonesia
- USPTO
- EUIPO
- WIPO Global Brand Database
- relevant national trademark databases
- other authoritative trademark sources available through research tools

For every material result record:

- jurisdiction
- mark
- owner
- class/category
- status
- filing/registration information if visible
- source
- evidence date
- evidence URL

### Evidence classification

Use exactly:

**VERIFIED**  
Directly confirmed through an authoritative source/tool.

**SEARCH-OBSERVED**  
Observed through public search results but not authoritative confirmation.

**UNVERIFIED**  
Could not be confirmed.

**REQUIRES PROFESSIONAL CLEARANCE**  
A legal conclusion cannot be made from ordinary research.

### CRITICAL RULE

Ordinary web research is NOT trademark clearance.

Never say:

- “trademark is clear”
- “trademark is safe”
- “legally available”

unless an actual authoritative/legal basis supports that exact statement.

Even an apparently clean candidate must be marked:

**REQUIRES PROFESSIONAL CLEARANCE**

unless professional clearance has actually occurred.
