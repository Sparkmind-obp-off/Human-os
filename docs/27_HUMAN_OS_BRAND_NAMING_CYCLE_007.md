## 3. Product Understanding

Human-OS is a persistent, voice-first personal operating layer for reasoning, coordination, memory, action, verification, and reflection. Its core loop is *Sense → Understand → Plan → Act → Verify → Remember → Reflect*. It coordinates fragmented digital systems while keeping the human as owner, authority, and final decision-maker. Presence, in Human-OS terms, means continuity of service — not simulated personhood.

The commercial brand that will represent Human-OS must therefore support: infrastructure, continuity, intelligence, coordination, capability, calm, trust, human agency, technical credibility, and long-term platform expansion. The brand must not depend on the words *AI*, *assistant*, *voice*, *memory*, or *automation*. Human-OS itself is not being renamed; the new brand names the commercial identity that carries Human-OS to market.

KAERVAX is out of scope for Cycle 007 and is not evaluated, compared, or referenced beyond this statement of exclusion.

## 22. Strategic Learning

Cycle 007 confirms the strategic principle that motivated its own creation. The unresolved constraint after Cycle 006 was never *insufficient candidate quality* — it was *the absence of authoritative clearance*. Opening a seventh generative batch would not have addressed that constraint; it would have deferred it while accumulating additional strategic cost. The disciplined outcome — declaring *NO FINAL BRAND LOCK — PROFESSIONAL CLEARANCE PENDING* and producing a clean handoff packet — preserves the strength of the Cycle 006 shortlist and directs project energy to the actual next-step activity: professional engagement.

Cycle 007 also reinforces the operating principle **SEARCH-SPARSE ≠ TRADEMARK-CLEAR**. Public-web sparsity for any of KAEVOS, KITHVANE, or THELVRA does not lower the clearance bar. A candidate that appears clean in ordinary search may still carry a material registry, phonetic, or cross-class risk that only qualified counsel can characterise.

Finally, Cycle 007 makes explicit that *not locking is a valid outcome*. The program's goal is a defensible commercial identity, not a completed naming exercise. A defensible identity requires professional evidence; that evidence has not yet been produced; therefore no lock is issued.
