## 1. Purpose

This document is the controlled register for actual KAEVOS logo candidates.

It deliberately does **not** declare a final logo. Candidates may only advance when visual evidence exists for structure, monochrome performance, small-size behavior, product context, and similarity concerns.

## 2. Candidate Generation Contract

The first exploration batch should produce approximately 24–36 raw concepts across six territories:

- A — Controlled K
- B — Continuous Signal
- C — Converging Intent
- D — Verified Transition
- E — Layered Continuity
- F — Directional Abstract

Every concept receives a persistent ID:

`KV-[TERRITORY]-[NUMBER]-[ITERATION]`

Examples:

- `KV-A-01-A`
- `KV-B-03-A`
- `KV-C-02-A`
- `KV-D-04-A`
- `KV-E-01-A`
- `KV-F-03-A`

No concept should be considered a candidate merely because an image-generation model produced it.

## 3. Candidate Lifecycle

`RAW → REFINE → STRESS_TEST → HOLD → CLEARANCE_REVIEW → FINAL_CANDIDATE`

A concept may move backward at any point.

`REJECTED` is terminal unless a materially different reconstruction creates a new concept identity.

## 6. First-Pass Screening

Before any visual refinement, every generated concept must pass these gates:

- distinctive enough to warrant further investigation,
- understandable as a standalone symbol,
- not dependent on color,
- not dependent on 3D or gradients,
- not obviously generic AI/SaaS imagery,
- capable of clean vector construction,
- compatible with KAEVOS tone,
- compatible with human-control positioning.

Any failure results in `REJECTED` or a documented reconstruction.

## 7. Stress-Test Protocol

Surviving concepts must be rendered/tested at:

- 16 px
- 24 px
- 32 px
- 48 px
- 96 px
- large presentation scale

Test each in:

- black on white,
- white on black,
- neutral dark UI,
- signal-accent UI.

Color must not rescue a weak silhouette.

## 8. Product Context Test

Every surviving candidate must be placed in at least:

1. KAEVOS navigation/header
2. dashboard application icon
3. browser favicon
4. mobile home-screen icon
5. social avatar
6. login/landing hero
7. system-status interface

The symbol should remain recognizable without explanatory text.

## 9. Wordmark Test

Test:

**KAEVOS**

with:

- Inter
- Geist
- restrained custom spacing
- symbol-left lockup
- symbol-only lockup

Do not modify the core symbol merely to make it fit a fashionable wordmark.

## 10. Similarity and Collision Log

For every concept entering `STRESS_TEST`, document:

- visually similar marks discovered,
- category/industry overlap,
- distinctive geometry shared with another identity,
- likely confusion risks,
- search terms used,
- source/reference,
- date checked.

This is an exploratory similarity log, not legal clearance.
